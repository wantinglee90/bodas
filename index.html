Dress Code

Black Tie Optional

Think glamorous. Tuxedos and gowns are warmly encouraged — a sharp suit or elegant dress is equally welcome. December in México City is mild, so dress for the evening ahead.

A Grown-Up Affair

Our celebration is adults only, with the exception of immediate family. For those traveling with little ones, however, México City is a spectacular family destination — world-class museums, ancient ruins, and beautiful parks make it well worth extending your trip.

☀

The Neighbourhood

The venue sits in Centro Histórico, the ancient heart of the city and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Most guests will want to base themselves nearby — our favourites:

Roma Norte — the city's creative pulse, lined with restaurants, cafés, and art nouveau architecture
Condesa — leafy, unhurried, beautiful art deco streets and parkside dining
Polanco — polished boutiques and excellent restaurants worthy of extending your trip to explore
